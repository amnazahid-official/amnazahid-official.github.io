Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit amnazahid-official.github.io (the “website”), why it is collected, and the choices you have. The website is run by Amna Zahid, a freelance marketing professional based in Pakistan.

1. Information you choose to share

  • Contact form: your name, email address, business or brand, budget range and message.
  • Chat assistant: if you choose to leave your details, your name, email address and what you need help with.
  • Bookings and messages: when you book through Calendly, or contact by WhatsApp or email, the details you provide on those services.

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry, prepare for a consultation, and provide the services you request.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running.
  • On-device storage: the website stores a small note in your browser so the chat greeting is not shown repeatedly. It contains no personal information.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): if analytics are enabled, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may be used to understand how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed, device type, approximate location and anonymised interaction recordings. These tools run only after you click “Accept” on the cookie notice, and you can decline.

3. Services that help run the website

Your information is processed by these providers only as needed to deliver their service:

  • Formspree: delivers contact form and chat assistant submissions by email.
  • Calendly: handles consultation bookings.
  • GitHub: hosts the website.
  • WhatsApp and Gmail: used when you choose to message or email.
  • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity: website analytics, only with your consent.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. Some are based outside your country, including in the United States, so your information may be processed there.

4. What is never done with your information

Your information is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is not added to mailing lists without your permission.

5. How long information is kept

Inquiry details are kept only as long as needed to communicate with you and, if you become a client, to manage the working relationship and meet legal or accounting obligations. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time.

6. Legal basis (for visitors in the UK and EU)

Information is processed on the basis of your consent (for example, analytics and submitting a form), to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and for legitimate interests such as keeping the website secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict its use, request a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email email. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), loads no third-party scripts unless you consent to analytics, and uses a strict security policy that only allows its own code to run. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but reasonable steps are taken to protect your information.

9. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and their information is not knowingly collected.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions? Email email and you will receive a reply as soon as possible.
